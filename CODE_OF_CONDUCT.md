# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome differing viewpoints, give feedback in good faith, and focus discussion on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and publication of private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report concerns through the contact details in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate discretion.
